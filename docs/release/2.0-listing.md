## Description

This is not the official Microduck app. It is an independent app for people who own, or are waiting for, a Pollen Robotics Microduck.

PLAY
Three ways to control a duck, all on one screen:
- Drive: two thumb sticks, or a game controller, with the same speeds Pollen's own gamepad uses.
- Tell: type or say what you want, like "walk forward and sit down". The phone turns it into steps you can see before anything moves.
- Auto: ask the duck, and a model on your phone picks from the moves your duck really has. Every plan is shown first, with a Cancel button.
Stop is always on screen, and always goes first.

SIRI AND SHORTCUTS
"Stop the duck", "Sit the duck down" and "Stand the duck up" work from Siri, Spotlight, the Action button and the Shortcuts app.

A PRACTICE DUCK WITH REAL PHYSICS
No robot yet? The practice duck is a physics simulation that runs inside the app, using Pollen's own trained networks. Walk it, kick a ball, and try the Dock routine: it sits, scoots back on its seat and scoots out again, using the MicroDock's scoot networks.

MOVES
Author a motion keyframe by keyframe, or describe it and let a language model draft it. Every angle is checked against the robot's joint travel. Duck Cards turn a row of paper cards into a program a child can read.

STUDIO
For people who want to look inside: open the trained networks that drive the robot, see what each one reads, measure how often a move works across repeated runs, and compare two policies side by side. The app is careful about the difference between "it looked right once" and "it works".

YOUR OWN DUCK
The app finds your Microduck on your network or over Bluetooth, and talks to the software already on it.

HONEST ABOUT WHAT IT IS
Microduck Studio is an independent project. It is not made by, endorsed by, or affiliated with Pollen Robotics. Microduck is their robot; this is an independent owner's app for it. Policies released by Pollen are labelled as theirs, and are included under Apache-2.0. The MicroDock scoot networks are by the MicroDock's designer, also Apache-2.0.

Microduck, by Pollen Robotics: https://pollen-robotics.com/microduck/
